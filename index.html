What I do

I own implementations end to end. That starts in discovery, where a fragmented stack and a list of business workflows become integration requirements, data flows, and a phased plan. It continues through configuration, SIT and UAT, cutover, and hypercare, with scope held through SOWs and change requests and status reported to customer leadership.

Most of my work sits between an ERP and everything it has to talk to: trading partners over EDI and AS2, SFTP, or VAN; Shopify and other sales channels over APIs and webhooks; 3PL and WMS systems; and parcel carriers. On the presales side I run technical discovery, write the solution and the SOW, and stay on the account after go-live to scope the next phase.

I also design the LLM agents in PartnerLinQ's LinQ.IQ, built around the same configuration, mapping, and exception work I do by hand on customer programs.
